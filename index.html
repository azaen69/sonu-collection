FINANCIAL LITERACY

Understand before you borrow.

A loan isn't just about the EMI. Here's what you should know.

₹

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount you pay every month towards your loan.

%

Interest Matters

A higher interest rate means you'll generally pay more money over the lifetime of the loan.

↗

Tenure Matters

A longer tenure can reduce your monthly EMI, but it usually increases the total interest you pay.

BEFORE TAKING A LOAN

Ask yourself these questions.

✓ Can I comfortably afford the EMI?
✓ What is the total amount I'll repay?
✓ Is there a processing fee?
✓ Are there prepayment charges?
✓ Do I actually need this loan?
